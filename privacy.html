Refund Tracker privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026. Operator: Sunil Karki (United States). Contact: daichangsunil@gmail.com.

What Refund Tracker does

Refund Tracker helps you record expected refunds and compare them with credits from accounts you choose to connect. Bank updates can be delayed, and matches may require your review. We do not move money.

Information we process

We process your account name and email, expected refund details, selected bank account identifiers (including account type and masked account number), transaction amounts, dates, merchants and categories used for matching, and receipt images you choose to attach. When you pick a receipt or screenshot, the app reads its text on your phone to suggest the store and amount; that text is not sent to us or to our analytics or error-monitoring providers. We also process authentication and device records, notification tokens, and security events. Bank authentication is handled through Plaid and your bank; Refund Tracker does not ask you to provide bank passwords directly to us.

We use this information to provide refund tracking, matching, account security, support and notifications. Connecting a bank is optional; manual refund tracking remains available. Connecting an account authorizes the requested data access presented during the connection flow. Disconnecting stops further access through that connection after provider removal completes; it does not automatically delete previously saved refund records.

Service providers and sharing

Plaid provides bank connectivity and transaction data. Supabase provides authentication, database and file storage. Resend processes authentication and account emails. Expo and Apple process push delivery when enabled. Sentry receives error reports when the app fails (error type, the screen's route name, app and operating-system version); amounts, merchants, receipt text, account details and credentials are removed before sending. PostHog receives anonymous usage events (for example, that a refund was saved or a screen was opened) under a random identifier that is not linked to your name or email; it receives no amounts, merchants, receipt content or bank data. On Android, receipt reading uses Google's on-device ML Kit, and Google Play services may send Google device, app and performance information about that feature; receipt images and text stay on the phone. On iPhone, receipt reading uses Apple's on-device text recognition. Provider-specific retention and processing also depend on their own terms.

We do not sell Plaid-sourced consumer data or share it for advertising. Information is processed for the app's operation, at your direction, and when legally required. An export you share is sent to the destination you choose and is then subject to that destination's handling.

Storage and security

Refund Tracker stores data in the United States. Hosted Supabase customer data is encrypted at rest; its HTTP APIs use TLS. Provider access tokens receive additional application-level encryption. The app uses operating-system-protected app storage and deletes temporary exports after sharing, with a launch cleanup for interrupted sharing. These protections reduce risk but do not guarantee security.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and refund data while needed to provide your account and tracking, unless you request deletion or a shorter implemented retention rule applies. You can request account deletion in the app. Deletion proceeds asynchronously through bank revocation, file removal and account-data removal; a request is not complete until the server confirms it. Security-event retention is configured in source for 90 days. Export downloads are short-lived; expiry and deletion are different, and failed cleanup must be retried.

Our Supabase Pro setup uses daily database backups; Supabase documents a seven-day recovery window for these backups. Database backups do not include receipt-file contents stored through the Storage API. Deleted database records may remain in existing backups until those backups expire under the provider’s retention practices; account deletion does not immediately erase backups. The recovery window is not a guarantee that every provider copy is physically erased within seven days. We aim to complete account deletion within 30 days of your request, after which remaining copies exist only in backups until they expire (about seven days). Refund history is kept only while your account exists. Receipt files are deleted with your account and are not kept in separate backups.

Your choices

You can choose which eligible accounts to connect, disconnect a bank, manage notification permission, remove receipts and refund records, request an export, and request account deletion. Contact the operator through the public support contact to request help or exercise applicable privacy rights; identity verification may be needed. Requests must not include passwords, authentication codes or bank credentials.

Beta testing

During a beta, testing can end at any time. When a beta ends, the app may stop working and we will delete beta accounts, disconnect connected banks and remove refund records as described above, unless you ask to keep your account.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel. Public support email: daichangsunil@gmail.com. Effective date: October 29, 2026.

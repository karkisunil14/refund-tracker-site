Three ways to add a refund

Pick the original purchase from your account, add a receipt or screenshot, or type the details. All three end on one confirmation screen.

Twice-daily checks

We look for a matching posted credit twice a day. Partial, uncertain or repeated amounts are shown to you for review instead of being guessed.

Read-only bank access

Bank connections use Plaid. You log in on your bank's own screen; Refund Tracker never sees your bank password and never moves money.

You stay in control

Connect only the accounts you choose, disconnect any time, export your data, or delete your account from inside the app.
